01

Principles

iThe work is the picture

Fighters, skins and textures are the only imagery. The interface never decorates around them.

iiRules, not boxes

Structure comes from hairlines and alignment. A card or a shadow has to earn its place.

iiiFacts in mono

Versions, sizes, frames, hashes and file names are set in DM Mono and align in columns.

ivGold means yours

The accent marks what you changed, chose or installed. Primary actions are ink.

vEvery action has a key

Keys show beside the actions they trigger. The mouse is optional.

04

Lines, corners, depth

line · 1pxrows
line-strong · 1pxframes, heads
ink · 2pxsections

Three weights only. A 2px ink rule opens a section; nothing else is that heavy.

Tables and plates are square. Controls and panes take 4px. No pills; that's a deliberate break from the site.

One shadow, for things that float above the page: menus, the command bar, dialogs. Panes sit flat.

A flat ground, one hairline for the floor, and labels in the corners in mono. The model gets the warm plate colour behind it in Paper and near-black in Gallery. No glow, no vignette, no floor reflection.

Small plates for lists and libraries. They hold still until you hover.

08

Layout

tgg-editor's shell, kept: one sidebar of places, detail in a pane column.

A place. The sidebar holds places (Melee, Library, Mods) and recent edits, never a place's contents. It hides whole; its toggle never moves.

A subject. Opening a fighter or a costume gives it the whole page: the stage, a timeline, and a column of panes that fold to their headers. Escape goes back up.

A list. A list's selection opens in the same pane column, docked. So there's one kind of side panel, never a second sidebar.

10

Not in this language

noGlass and glow

Blur, backdrop filters, coloured glows, vignettes.

noGradient icon tiles

Mods are named, not given app-store icons.

noPills for everything

Tags, statuses and buttons as rounded chips.

noTop navigation

Places live in the sidebar; the top row says where you are.

noA second sidebar

Detail is a pane column, not another nav.

noDecorative motion

Nothing animates that didn't change.
